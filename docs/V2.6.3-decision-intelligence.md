# V2.6.3 Decision Intelligence

## Design Review and Scope

This release prioritizes an explainable purchase ceiling, not more scores.

| Class | Metrics | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A: primary | Verdict, profit, ROI, Max Buy Price, qualitative Risk | Implemented |
| B: details | Break-even, required sale price for both targets, targets, tax assumption, IQR volatility, sample competition, reasons | Implemented |
| C: internal/API | Separate target-profit and target-ROI prices, binding max-buy constraint, gates | Implemented |
| D: future | Personal days to sell, capital efficiency, scenario calculator, historical trends | Deferred |

Market Data Quality keeps High/Medium/Low. No success percentage, extra
Opportunity Confidence, STRONG BUY tier, or duplicate velocity score is added.
Existing market-wide sale intervals are not personal holding-time estimates.
No additional provider requests or extra snapshot writes are required.

## Exact Model

All values are USD. Let P = item-only sale price, B = buyer-paid shipping,
C = pre-tax purchase price, t = assumed purchase tax rate, f = selling fee rate,
a = promoted listing rate, F = fixed order fee, S = seller shipping, K = packaging,
M = minimum profit, r = minimum ROI. Rates in these formulas are fractions.

```
L = C(1+t)
A = P(1-f-a) + B(1-f) - F - S - K
profit = A - L
ROI = profit / L * 100                 (L > 0; otherwise N/A)
max buy = min(A-M, A/(1+r)) / (1+t)
break even P = max(0, (L+F+S+K-B(1-f))/(1-f-a))
target profit P = max(0, (L+M+F+S+K-B(1-f))/(1-f-a))
target ROI P = max(0, (L(1+r)+F+S+K-B(1-f))/(1-f-a))
required P = max(target profit P, target ROI P)
```

Max buy rounds DOWN to cents; required prices round UP. A negative max buy
is N/A with an explicit infeasibility reason, not a fictitious $0 opportunity.
Negative, nonfinite inputs and f+a >= 1 fail closed. Small floating-point noise
is removed at threshold boundaries without rounding profit to whole cents.
ROI uses tax-inclusive acquisition cost, not pre-tax cost.

Market quotes are shipping-inclusive: subtract modeled buyer shipping to obtain
P before calculating gross proceeds P+B. Manual sale-price overrides are P.
Pricing excludes Best Offer unknown final prices and undated/future/>90-day Sold.
At least three confirmed recent Sold prices are needed for decision ceilings.
Unknown source shipping cannot establish a reliable shipping-inclusive quote.
Counts retain dated matched sales, including Best Offer, as activity evidence.

Assumptions: default tax 0%, default targets $25/40%; category fee defaults are
estimates, not a verified current eBay fee schedule. Fees on marketplace-collected
tax, fee tiers, returns and actual advertising billing bases are not modeled.
Users must verify these costs before purchasing. Product inputs support target
and purchase-tax overrides. Deal Scan uses defaults; its existing filters remain
display filters, not custom purchase targets.

## Gates and Reasons

Product and Deal Scan both use `calculateEconomics` and `buildDecision`.
The legacy exported Deal verdict function remains only for older consumers.

Missing acceptable match, provider completeness, freshness, uncapped market data,
High/Medium quality, reliable confirmed prices, calculable profit/ROI or a usable
shipping estimate produces MAYBE with explicit gate reasons.
With gates satisfied: nonpositive profit or ROI <20% produces SKIP; profit and
ROI meeting the configured targets plus >=3 Sold produces BUY; otherwise MAYBE.
Deal Score ranks attractiveness but does not override the shared verdict.

Risk is qualitative, not a loss probability: gate failures, bulky shipping or
volatile pricing are High; estimated shipping, unknown volatility or crowded
sample competition are Medium; otherwise Low. Bulky/volatile risk is advisory,
not an automatic SKIP. Unknown packed weight without a manual shipping quote
blocks BUY; ordinary estimated shipping does not.

Volatility requires >=5 confirmed prices: IQR/median <=0.2 Stable, <=0.5 Moderate,
otherwise Volatile. These are uncalibrated descriptive thresholds, not forecasts.
Competition uses uncapped quality-gated active/sold90 samples: <=1 Low competition,
<=3 Balanced, >3 Crowded. It is explicitly not a full-market inventory measure.
Days to Sell and Capital Efficiency remain null: market sample data cannot
justify an individual holding period or monthly profit projection.

## UI and Storage

Deal cards prioritize verdict, profit, ROI, purchase ceiling and risk; matching,
market and fees are collapsed. Product results share the same intelligence view.
Target-profit and target-ROI prices collapse into one required-price value.

`0004_decision_intelligence.sql` adds nullable `decision_json` TEXT, containing
schemaVersion 1. Older rows, malformed JSON and unsupported versions read as null.
No backfill, new index, additional table, or extra write is necessary. Existing
API fields remain; `decisionIntelligence` is additive. Old Watchlist/history
records have N/A new metrics. Historical intelligence remains a later release.

## QA and Deployment

Run Node syntax checks, `npm test`, `git diff --check`, and Wrangler dry-run.
Tests cover inverse equations, simultaneous targets, tax, rounding, cost zero,
invalid fees/costs, missing data, gates, risk, outliers, snapshots and legacy rows.
The original 95 tests remain, with new pure-engine and migration coverage.
Live provider availability is not established by fixture tests.

Before production deployment:

1. Confirm Cloudflare authentication and D1 backup/recovery policy.
2. Apply `npx wrangler d1 migrations apply DB --remote` (migration 0004).
3. Run `npx wrangler deploy` and verify authenticated Product/Deal scans.
4. Confirm D1 decision JSON and old-row rendering; compare a second scan.
5. Verify today's opportunities, mobile UI, provider health and scheduled run.

No new Secrets or Variables are required. Keep existing V2.6.2 authentication,
provider keys, D1 binding and budget settings; never commit secret values.
V2.7 notifications should wait for this production regression verification and
an explicit policy for freshness, risky BUY results, deduplication and opt-in.
V2.6.4 should address fee/shipping assumptions and a shared-engine local scenario
calculator. V2.8 can add longitudinal price and availability evidence.
